About

GOOD NEIGHBOR is a 5C student project, built by a student for students.

Sometimes I dreaded going to the dining halls and I think this is a pretty universal experience

  • You don't feel like seeing people or socializing
  • You don't want to wait in a long ass line just to get food
  • The walk is way too long, even when the food sounds really good
  • You're studying at the library and don't want to pack everything up and leave

I've experienced these things myself, and I started wondering: If a service existed that could bring food directly to me, would I actually pay for it? I figured other students might feel the same way. That's why I'm building GOOD NEIGHBOR. It's a student to student delivery service that brings meals right to your dorm or wherever you are on campus, without having to ask a friend for a favor. It also creates more on campus job opportunities for students who want to earn some extra money.
