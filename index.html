About

GoodNeighbor is a 5C project, built by a student for students.

I got really sick once and didn't want to ask my friends to drop food off for me. So basically, I starved. That stuck with me.

Sometimes you just need food without the whole trip:

  • The line at the dining hall is too long
  • You don't feel like seeing or socializing with anyone
  • You just don't want to walk into the dining hall
  • You're studying at Honnold late at night and can't leave

I'm making GoodNeighbor to solve that. Students deliver to students, right to your dorm, so no one has to go hungry or ask for a favor.
